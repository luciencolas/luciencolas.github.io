About

International experience, field exposure and strategic analysis.

I am an international affairs professional with more than eight years of experience in economic diplomacy, international cooperation and institutional engagement.

From 2016 to 2020, I was based in Northeast China, where I headed the Charente-Maritime Department's representative office. Working with Chinese provincial and municipal authorities, French diplomatic missions, companies and institutional partners gave me direct exposure to China's political, economic and institutional environment.

Since 2022, I have worked at Expertise France on international cooperation involving French ministries, foreign governments and international organisations. My current work provides growing exposure to strategic sectors including energy, critical minerals, emerging technologies and industrial value chains.

Alongside my professional experience, I have developed a long-standing interest in geopolitics, political economy, the history of ideas and long-term power dynamics. This portfolio brings together independent analysis on China and the Asia-Pacific, geoeconomics, economic security, strategic industries and great-power competition.
